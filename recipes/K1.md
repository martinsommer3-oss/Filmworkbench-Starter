# K1 — Dialogue with close-ups

[Watch the S2 example](https://github.com/martinsommer3-oss/Filmworkbench-Starter/releases/download/v0.1.0/Filmworkbench_K1_S2.mp4) · 28.54 seconds · 1952×1088 · 24 fps

## Recipe

Establish two characters, their positions and the relevant props in one wide view. Plan close-ups from that camera side, then return to the wide. Continue the accepted AV latent.

## Known limits

Eye lines can remain too frontal; a hand gesture is not proof of a successful grasp. Voice identity can drift.

This is a selected development example with positive user feedback, not a measured success rate. The feedback file predates some bookkeeping updates; no new blanket approval of every variant is implied.
